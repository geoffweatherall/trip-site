# Model shops

Plastic-model and hobby shops for Tokyo. Numbers 1 to 7 come from the *2026-japan-model-stores* sheet, and 8 to 10 were added from a web search. The map is separate from the [Tokyo sightseeing map](maps.md). The ★ is where we're staying.

**Akihabara is the main hub**: seven of the ten shops are within a short walk of each other. A sensible order is Yodobashi, then Radio Kaikan (Yellow Submarine and Yesabu), then Volks and Surugaya, then TamTam and Leonardo to the north. This order is my suggestion and I haven't walked it. Yurakucho, Shimbashi and Oji are each a separate stop.

| <a id="shop-9"></a>9 | **Yesabu** | Akihabara | A wide selection of scale and character kits | Check | Inside Akihabara Radio Kaikan (the pin is the building). "Yesabu" is also a common nickname for the Yellow Submarine chain (#3), so this may be the same shop **(check)**. | Web search |

Tax-free means foreign visitors can shop without consumption tax (bring your passport). "Check" means I couldn't confirm it. Opening hours and stock change, so check before you go.
