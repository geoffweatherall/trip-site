# Maps

Each pin has the **same number as its group** in that place's list. Orange circles are fine-weather groups and blue squares are wet-weather groups. The ★ is where we're staying. **M** marks a subway station, **T** a train station and **B** a bus stop (their names show when you zoom in, and the list under each map has them all). A pin marks the main spot of its group, and most groups have several stops. Tap a pin for a link to Google Maps. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
